# Store listing copy (original)

**App name:** StrideMate – Step Counter

**Short description (80 chars):** Count your steps and walk together with friends, family and teams. Always free.

**Full description:**

Walking is better together. StrideMate counts your steps, cheers you on, and turns everyday walks into friendly competition with the people you care about.

**Start right away.** Open the app and start counting. No account is required, and StrideMate never asks for your location.

**See your progress.** Today's steps and goal progress are front and centre, with distance and an estimated calorie burn just below. Browse your day, week, month and year, and check your personal records whenever you like.

**Walk with your people.** Create a private group for friends, family, coworkers or your club, and invite people with a code or link that you control. Chat, send quick cheers, and trade playful taunts (anyone can mute them). Daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards always show where you stand, even in a group of thousands.

**Start a challenge.** Pick dates or keep it going, set a time zone so everyone plays fair, and try Mystery Mode: today's steps stay secret until midnight, then the reveal.

**Stay motivated.** Set a daily goal, plan Rest Days that protect your streak, unlock achievements, and race Chill Bot, Active Bot or your own Yesterday Bot.

**Bring your watch.** StrideMate works with your phone's motion sensor and with Health Connect or Apple Health, so steps from many popular watches, bands and rings can count too, without double counting.

**Share your story.** Monthly and yearly StrideMate Recaps turn your walking into a shareable card, with your own photo if you like.

**Your data, your call.**

- Choose who sees your steps, group by group.
- Export your data or delete your account at any time.

**Free. Every feature.** No subscriptions, no paywalls.
